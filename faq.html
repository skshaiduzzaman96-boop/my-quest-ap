Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the questions we hear most often about System Quest.

Is System Quest free to play?

Yes. System Quest is completely free — there's nothing to purchase to start playing, and all core levels are accessible from the start.

Do I need to create an account?

No account is required. Just open the game and start playing — your progress is saved automatically on your device.

Will I lose my progress if I clear my browser data or switch devices?

Yes. Progress is stored locally on the device and browser you're playing in, so clearing site data, switching browsers, or moving to a new device will reset your save. We recommend not clearing your browser's site data if you want to keep your progress.

What is "Claim Reward" and is it required?

Claim Reward is an optional bonus you can trigger at certain points in the game for an extra boost. It's never required to complete a level — it's simply there for players who want it.

Which devices and browsers does System Quest support?

System Quest runs in any modern mobile or desktop browser, and is also available as an installable app. If you notice any issues on a specific device, please let us know via the contact details on our About page.

Does System Quest show ads?

System Quest may display ads served through Google AdSense/AdMob to support ongoing development. See our Privacy Policy for details on how advertising data is handled.

I found a bug — how do I report it?

We appreciate bug reports. Please reach out through the contact information on our About page with a short description of what happened and what device/browser you were using.

Will more levels or content be added?

System Quest is actively developed by LevelXPath Games, and we plan to keep expanding it over time. Check back for updates.
