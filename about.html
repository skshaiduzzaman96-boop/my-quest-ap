System Quest

by LevelXPath Games

What is System Quest?

System Quest is a free browser-based adventure and puzzle game built for quick, casual play sessions. It runs entirely in your browser — no download, no account, and no installation required. Your progress is saved automatically on your device so you can pick up right where you left off.

How to Play

Start a new game from the main menu, or continue your saved progress if you've played before. Follow the on-screen instructions for each level. You can adjust settings such as sound and difficulty from the in-game menu at any time.

Saving Your Progress

System Quest automatically saves your progress locally in your browser. Because saves are stored on your device, clearing your browser's site data or switching devices will reset your progress. We recommend not clearing your browser cache for this site if you want to keep your saved game.

Rewards

Certain in-game rewards can be claimed by watching a short video. This is optional and only appears when you choose to claim a bonus — it is never required to progress through the game.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is System Quest free?
Yes, System Quest is completely free to play.

Do I need to create an account?
No account is needed. Just open the page and start playing.

Does it work offline?
The game needs an internet connection to load initially, but gameplay itself is lightweight and works well even on slower connections.

I found a bug — how do I report it?
