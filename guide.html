How to Play System Quest

Everything you need to know to start your run, climb the levels, and make the most of every session.

Getting Started

System Quest opens straight into the studio intro, then drops you into the main menu. No installation, sign-up, or account is required — the game runs entirely in your browser (or as an installed app if you added it from the Play Store), and your progress is saved automatically on your own device as you play.

Core Gameplay

Each level presents a self-contained challenge. Your goal is to clear the level's objective using the tools and mechanics introduced as you progress. Later levels combine earlier mechanics in new ways, so paying attention to how each stage works will make the harder stages easier to solve.

  • Tap or click to interact with objects and controls on screen.
  • Each completed level unlocks the next one in sequence.
  • Difficulty increases gradually, so it's worth mastering early levels before rushing ahead.

Progression System

Your progress through System Quest is tracked locally and picks up right where you left off the next time you open the game. There's no timer forcing you to finish in one sitting — take breaks whenever you like.

Rewards

At certain points in the game you'll see a Claim Reward option. Selecting it triggers a short charging sequence, after which your bonus is added to your run. This is entirely optional and is there for players who want an extra boost — it's never required to finish a level.

Tip: If a level feels stuck, try re-reading the on-screen prompt for that stage — most levels hint at the mechanic you're expected to use before introducing a twist on it.

Saving Your Progress

System Quest stores your progress locally on the device and browser you're playing in. Clearing your browser data, switching devices, or uninstalling the app will reset your save, since progress isn't currently synced to an account or the cloud.
