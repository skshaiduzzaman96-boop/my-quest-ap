Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026 — Applies to System Quest, developed by LevelXPath Games.

This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you play System Quest, how it is used, and the choices available to you. We built System Quest to work without requiring an account, and we try to collect as little information as possible.

Information We Do Not Collect

System Quest does not require you to create an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address, or any other personal contact information to play the game.

Local Game Data

Your progress, settings, and in-game stats are stored locally on your device using your browser's local storage. This data stays on your device, is not transmitted to our servers, and is removed automatically if you clear your browser data or uninstall the app.

Advertising

System Quest may display advertising served through Google AdSense and/or Google AdMob. These services may use cookies, device identifiers, or similar technologies to serve ads, including personalised ads based on your interests, and to measure ad performance.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this and other sites.
  • You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can learn more about how Google uses data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Third-Party Services

System Quest is hosted on GitHub Pages and may use Google AdSense/AdMob for advertising. Each of these third parties operates under its own privacy policy, and we encourage you to review them for details on how they handle data.

Children's Privacy

System Quest is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can address it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the game, our practices, or legal requirements. Any changes will be posted on this page.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out via the contact details on our About page.
